Sprayer FAQs

What tractor horsepower does a Victair Mistifier need?

From a low 35 HP up to 70 HP, with a power take off requirement of 540 RPM. The requirement depends on the size of the air discharge volutes; 1”, 2”, 3” and spray tower volutes are available, with 3” standard.

Source: Victair Mistifier page, hfhauff.com (archived 2012)

What tank sizes and pumps are available?

VICTAIR Mistifier sprayers are available in 200, 300, 400, 500 gallon sizes, with piston diaphragm pumps of 10, 15, 25, 36 GPM. Optional centrifugal pumps are also available.

Source: Victair Mistifier page, hfhauff.com (archived 2012). Confirm current sizes
