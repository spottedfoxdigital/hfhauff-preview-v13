Engineering Behind the Inversion 25

H.F. Hauff engineering: the tower, blade and braced base come together in a machine for low crops.

  • Low-crop configuration. A 25-foot tower paired with an 18-foot blade.
  • Blade specification. 150 hp blade specification supplied for the Inversion 25.
  • Tower positioning. The design views show upright, inclined and horizontal tower positions.
  • Base integration. A wheeled chassis and angled supports form the base assembly.
